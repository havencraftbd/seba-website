Our Origin

HavenCraft started in early 2025 when a few builders, redstone players, and moderators got tired of choosing between high-ping overseas servers and local setups that shut down after a few weeks. Our goal was to keep a permanent survival server running for players in Bangladesh.

Alongside standard survival, we run seasonal events for Pohela Boishakh and Eid, player shops at spawn, and group builds with players joining from Dhaka, Chattogram, Rajshahi, and other districts.

With Java and Bedrock crossplay on by default, friends on PC, mobile, and console all play in the same world and use the same economy.
