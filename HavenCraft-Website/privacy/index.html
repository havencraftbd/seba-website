Privacy Policy

How HavenCraft collects, processes, and safeguards your player data across our server, store, and website.

Information we collect

4 categories

Account & connection data

Connection

When you connect to HavenCraft, the server logs your Minecraft username, UUID, and IP address. We need this data for login authentication, DDoS protection, session stability, and ban enforcement.

In-game gameplay & leaderboards

Gameplay

We record gameplay stats such as playtime, balance, kills, deaths, vote counts, and block metrics to run public leaderboards, player profiles, and the server economy. Public in-game chat is logged for moderation.

Store & transaction records

Financial Privacy

Payments go through third-party providers (bKash, Nagad, cards). HavenCraft never sees or stores your bank passwords, card numbers, or mobile wallet PINs. We log only transaction IDs (TrxID), sender numbers/methods as submitted, usernames, and timestamps to verify orders.

Discord & support communications

Community

If you link Discord or open a support ticket, we store your Discord User ID or handle to assign rank roles and reply to your ticket. We never access your private Discord chats or server lists outside official HavenCraft channels.

How we use and protect data

3 commitments

Service operation & anti-cheat

Operations

Your data is used to keep the server running, prevent griefing, update leaderboards, and catch hacked clients. Anti-cheat and moderation tools check movement and combat packets in real time to keep play fair.

No data sales

No Data Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade player records, emails, or personal data to third parties or marketing brokers. Your activity on HavenCraft is never used for ad targeting.

Encryption & strict access control

Security

Web traffic uses SSL/TLS encryption, and database access is gated by permission rules and environment API keys. Only authorized senior administrators can view infrastructure logs for security audits.

Third-party services & infrastructure

3 providers

Supabase cloud database

Database

We use Supabase (a managed PostgreSQL host) to store player stats, vote timestamps, support tickets, and profile data. Row Level Security (RLS) restricts unauthorized access.

Cloudflare CDN & edge protection

Infrastructure

Web traffic to havencraft.pro is routed through Cloudflare to provide fast global asset caching, SSL certificate management, and mitigation against automated bot assaults and Layer 7 DDoS attacks.

Skins, server ping & analytics

Utility

Player avatars and 3D heads are rendered via public skin caches (mc-heads.net / crafthead.net), while real-time server status uses mcsrvstat.us. We use anonymized Google Analytics with IP masking solely to measure visitor volume and optimize web performance.

Player rights & governance

3 standards

Data access & deletion rights

Player Rights

You can request a summary of data tied to your account or ask us to delete non-essential profile data. Email us or open a private Discord ticket to start a request. Security logs and active bans are kept to protect the server.

Children's privacy & COPPA guidance

Under 13

HavenCraft does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent. If a parent or guardian finds that a child shared personal details here, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Policy revisions & transparency

Governance

We update this policy when we add features, change security practices, or meet new legal requirements. Major updates are posted in Discord announcements, and continuing to use HavenCraft accepts the updated policy.

Have data privacy or account questions?

For questions about stored data, profile updates, or deletion requests, contact our team on Discord or by email.
