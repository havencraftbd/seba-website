Server Guide

Wiki & Commands

Beginner-friendly guide to all features and commands

Click on any section below to see all the commands and details for that specific feature.

Explore Features
/register [password] [password] or /reg First time playing: You must register your account to protect it. Pick a secret password and type it twice. Example: /register MySecret123 MySecret123
/login [password] or /l Returning players: Every time you join the server again, you need to log in with your password. Example: /login MySecret123
/rtp or /wild Randomly teleport far away into the survival world so you can start building safely.
/spawn Return to the server's main spawn area safely.
/tpa [player] Send a request to teleport to another player. They can type /tpaccept or /tpdeny.
/sethome [name] Save your current location. You can type /home [name] later to teleport back here instantly!
/warp afk or /afkzone Teleport to the AFK Zone. You can stand here while away from your keyboard and earn passive rewards!
Spawners Mine spawners using a Silk Touch pickaxe. If you place multiple spawners of the same type next to each other, they will automatically stack into one! Right click them to upgrade.
/settings Open the settings menu to toggle TPA requests, Global Chat, Private Messages, the Scoreboard, Night Vision, and more.
/lb View the server leaderboard for Playtime, Kills, Deaths, etc.
/stats or /stats [player] View your gameplay statistics (playtime, kills, deaths, placed/broken blocks). Add a player's name (e.g. /stats player) to check someone else.
/sit, /crawl, /lay, /layback, /bellyflop, /spin Character emotes you can use anywhere in the world.
Golden Shovel Use a Golden Shovel to claim land. Right-click one corner of your base, then right-click the opposite corner to block other players from editing inside.
/trust [player] Give your friend permission to build, open doors, and access chests inside your claimed land.
/untrust [player] Remove a player's access from your land immediately.
/abandonclaim Delete the claim you are standing in. Warning: Anyone will be able to break your blocks.
/claimslist See a list of all places you have claimed and their coordinates.
/shop Open the main server shop to buy blocks, food, gear, or sell farm drops.
/sell Open a quick menu to drop survival items in and sell them immediately.
/price or /worth Open a category menu showing item prices so you know what each drop is worth.
/bal or /money Check how much money you currently have.
/baltop View the richest players on the server.
/pay [player] [amount] Transfer your money to another player.
/ah Open the Auction House to buy items listed by other players.
/ah sell [price] Hold an item in your hand and run this command to list it on the Auction House.
/shard Open the Shard Dashboard GUI to access your Profile, Daily Claims, Daily Quests, Rewards, Transaction History, and Top List.
/shard balance or /shard bal Check how many shards you currently have in your account balance.
/shard top View the top 10 shard holders on the server.
/shard history View your personal transaction log and recent shard activity.
/shard pay [player] [amount] Transfer shards from your account to another player.
/shard withdraw [amount] Withdraw shards from your account balance as physical items in your inventory.
/shard deposit Deposit physical shard items held in your hand back into your account balance.
/team create [name] Create your own team as its leader.
/team invite [player] Invite a player to join your team.
/team join [name] Accept an invitation and join a team.
/team chat Toggle team chat so only your teammates can read your messages.
/team ally [team] Send an alliance request to another team. Allies cannot damage each other.
