Chat & Community

6 rules

Respect the community

Core policy

Treat all players with respect. Toxic behavior, intentional harassment, hate speech, racism, personal attacks, or doxxing are prohibited across chat, private messages, signs, and books.

No advertising

Mute / Ban

Promoting other Minecraft servers, external gaming communities, unapproved Discord invites, personal social media, or commercial websites is prohibited, including unsolicited DM ads.

No spamming

Mute

Repeating the same message, flooding chat, pasting large unicode art, or typing in all caps is not allowed. Keep chat readable.

Use appropriate language

Warning / Mute

Both English and Bangla are welcome. Offensive, hateful, sexually explicit, or discriminatory language is not allowed. Keep conversations appropriate for all ages.

No harassment or bullying

Temporary ban

Intentionally insulting, belittling, stalking, or targeting anyone is prohibited. If another player asks you to stop bothering them or leave their area, respect their boundary. Continued harassment is penalized strictly.

No impersonation

Temporary ban

Pretending to be another player, a content creator, or a HavenCraft staff member using similar names, skins, or deceitful claims is strictly prohibited.

In-Game & Survival

6 rules

Seasonal gameplay system

Season policy

HavenCraft operates on a dynamic seasonal system. Specific survival mechanics, such as griefing guidelines, stealing allowances, or custom events, may adjust depending on the active season.

No cheating or hacks

Immediate ban

Hacks, x-ray texture packs, modified cheat clients, auto-clickers, baritone, freecam, or any tools giving unfair advantages are strictly prohibited. Both automated anti-cheat and staff logs are active 24/7.

Newbie protection

7-day shield

Do not attack, hunt, or kill players who joined HavenCraft within the last 7 days unless they hit you first. Give new players time to settle in.

PvP system and claims

PvP policy

PvP is enabled outside claimed areas. Stay inside your claim or safezones to avoid combat. Entering wilderness zones is at your own risk, and combat logging is not allowed.

Bugs, glitches and duping

Immediate ban

Using duplication glitches, chunk reset exploits, or economy loopholes is forbidden. Do not abuse bugs; report them to staff on Discord for an in-game bounty.

No real-money trading

Permanent ban

Real-money trading (selling in-game items, Shards, claimed land, or accounts for bKash, Nagad, or cash) outside the official HavenCraft store is prohibited to prevent fraud.

Building & Farming

4 rules

Spawn proximity buffer

Land removal

Do not build bases, dig large craters, or claim land too close to the main Spawn area without staff permission. Keep spawn borders clear for new players.

No inappropriate builds

Demolition & ban

Offensive, sexually explicit, or hate-symbol builds are prohibited and removed immediately without reimbursement.

Lag prevention and farm limits

Performance

Do not build AFK farms or machines that drop server TPS. Heavy looping redstone clocks and unauthorized chunk loaders are not allowed. Follow the server mob caps.

Community builds and landmarks

Zero griefing

Do not grief, alter, or loot community builds, public roads, event arenas, Nether hubs, or server landmarks. Follow any posted guidelines for shared community projects.

Punishment System

In case of a rule violation, staff may apply any of these disciplinary stages based on severity:

Stage 01

Warning

Verbal / Recorded

Issued for minor chat slip-ups, accidental boundary errors, or first-time minor infractions.

Stage 02

Mute

Chat silence

Applied for chat spamming, disrespectful language, caps flooding, or advertising violations.

Stage 03

Temporary ban

1 to 14 days

Enforced for repeat offenses, newbie hunting, combat logging, scamming, or minor griefing.

Stage 04

Permanent ban

Indefinite

Mandatory for hacked clients, x-ray, duping glitches, IRL trading, malicious griefing, or hate speech.

Staff discretion and common sense principle

Use common sense: These rules cannot list every situation. Any action that harms the server, exploits game mechanics, or targets players can still result in staff action.

Rule updates: Staff may add, remove, or update rules as needed to keep the server fair.

Need to report a violation or appeal a decision?

If you spot someone breaking the rules or think a punishment was issued by mistake, open a support ticket in our Discord.
