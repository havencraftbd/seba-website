Quick Answers

Common questions about joining, accounts, ping, and store orders.

How do I join on Java vs. Bedrock?

On Java Edition 1.21.11, connect to mc.havencraft.pro:2566. On Bedrock Edition (Mobile/Windows/Console), enter Server Address mc.havencraft.pro and Port 2025, or click Launch Bedrock above.

Can both premium and cracked accounts join?

Yes. Official Microsoft accounts and non-premium launchers both connect to the same Season 6 world using /register and /login protection.

What ping should I expect from Bangladesh?

The server runs 24/7 with South Asian routing so players in Dhaka, Chattogram, and across Bangladesh get low ping for survival and combat.

How are Store ranks & spawners delivered?

Complete your bKash or Nagad payment in the Store and enter your TrxID. Save or download your digital invoice and open a verification ticket in our Discord #store-orders or Support Desk.
